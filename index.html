About Kolan Hanmanth Reddy

Profile & Background

Kolan Hanmanth Reddy is a senior Indian National Congress (INC) leader representing the Quthbullapur constituency in Medchal-Malkajgiri district, Telangana. With extensive experience in business and civil contracts, he transitioned from practicing law to grassroots political engagement, becoming a prominent voice for community development and social welfare.

Education & Profession

Originally trained as an advocate, Kolan Hanmanth Reddy has built a distinguished career in business and civil contract works. Beyond his professional endeavors, he has consistently engaged in community service, particularly in healthcare access and community development initiatives.

Political Commitment

As a senior Congress leader in the Telangana Pradesh Congress Committee (TPCC), he contested the 2023 Telangana Legislative Assembly election from Quthbullapur, garnering significant support from the constituency. His campaign emphasized transparent representation, grassroots engagement, and inclusive development for all communities.
